Portal support

Email your college IT help desk (the address configured by your administrator) with your student ID and a description of the problem.

Administration office

For account activation, student ID corrections or admin access, visit the administration office with your ID card.

Office hours

Monday–Saturday, 9:30 AM – 4:30 PM (as configured by your college).
