What it does

Six services that used to live on notice boards and chat groups.

01

Events

Browse what is coming up, register before the deadline, and cancel if plans change. Seats are counted automatically, so an event never overfills.

02

Notes & resources

Lecture notes, question papers and lab manuals filtered by department, semester and subject. Student uploads are reviewed before they go public.

03

Announcements

Official notices, with pinned and department-specific posts, so you see what applies to you.

04

Clubs

See what each club does, who coordinates it and when it meets, then send a membership request.

05

Complaints

Raise an issue privately and follow it from submitted to resolved. Only you and the administration can read it.

06

Lost & found

Post what you lost or found, add a photo, and mark it recovered when it is back with its owner.

Who it is for

Built for students, run by the administration.

Students

  • Personal dashboard
  • Event registration
  • Share and download notes
  • Private complaint tracking

Administrators

  • Publish events and notices
  • Approve uploads and club requests
  • Resolve complaints with history
  • Live figures on one screen

Passwords are stored hashed, roles are checked on the server for every request, and uploaded files are only handed to people who are allowed to see them.
