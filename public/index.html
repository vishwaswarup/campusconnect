About Campus Connect

Campus services are often scattered across notice boards, chat groups and paper forms. Campus Connect replaces that with one transparent, secure portal for students and the administration.

  • Role-based access — students and admins see only what they should
  • Private complaints, protected files and hashed passwords
  • Responsive on phones, tablets and laptops
